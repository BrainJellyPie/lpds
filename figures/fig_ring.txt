Two-dimensional ring benchmark. Training points (black) are uniform on the annulus between radii 1.0 and 1.5 (dashed circles), and interior test points are uniform on the disc of radius 0.5 (dotted circle). (a) LPM and (b) the Isolation Forest anomaly score over the plane for one seed. (c) Distributions of both scores on held-out ring points and interior points, divided by the median score on ring points; the AUROC for separating interior from ring points is the mean over five seeds.
