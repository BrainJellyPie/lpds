Detection of synthetic extrapolation, averaged over the nine core datasets and five seeds. (a) AUROC between held-out test points and validation points shifted along the first principal component (PC1) of the standardized training features, as a function of the shift in units of the training PC1 standard deviation. (b) Mean LPM of the shifted points divided by the mean LPM of the test points for four shift directions. (c) AUROC at a shift of three PC1 standard deviations for each direction, with the same displacement length for all directions; the value of LPM is printed.
