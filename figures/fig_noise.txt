Effect of the noise structure on the association between risk scores and absolute error (five seeds). (a) Mean and standard deviation of the Spearman correlation of LPM on synthetic data with skewed features, as noise of increasing level is added uniformly, in the densest regions or in the sparsest regions of the feature space. (b) Mean correlation of LPM and two ambient-space scores at noise level 0.25. (c) Bike Sharing with the original target, a cross-fitted smooth signal plus randomly permuted residuals, the smooth signal alone and the smooth signal plus Gaussian noise whose standard deviation is a multiple of that of the original residuals; the test $R^2$ of the deployed model is printed above each bar.
